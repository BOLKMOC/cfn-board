CFN Board Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

ChangeFn LLC (CFN) runs board.changefn.com as an internal tool for its own owners and staff. This policy covers that site and its connection to CFN's own QuickBooks Online company. The site is not offered to the public or to other companies.

What we collect

  • Staff sign in with their CFN Google account. We keep their name, work email, and the pages they open (for security and audit).
  • From QuickBooks, read only: CFN's company name and monthly profit and loss totals by account. We do not read customers, invoices, bank accounts, or payment details, and we never write to QuickBooks.
  • Sales and call quality data from CFN's own systems, for CFN staff only.

How we use it

Only to show CFN's owners and managers how the business and each sales rep are doing. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with anyone outside CFN, and we do not use it for advertising.

How we protect it

Data is stored in a private database with access limited to the site's server. The QuickBooks refresh token is encrypted before it is stored and is replaced with a new one every time it is used. Every view by a manager is logged.

Your choices

An owner can disconnect QuickBooks at any time on the Finance page, or in QuickBooks under Apps. Data already pulled is removed on request.

Contact

Questions go to CFN management, or to the board's admin inbox.
