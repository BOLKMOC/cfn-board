CFN Board Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Change Financial Network (CFN) runs board.changefn.com for its own staff. This policy covers that site and its connection to CFN's QuickBooks Online company.

What we collect

  • Staff sign in with their CFN Google account. We keep their name, work email, and the pages they open (for security and audit).
  • From QuickBooks, read only: CFN's company name and monthly profit and loss totals by account. We do not read customers, invoices, bank accounts, or payment details.
  • Sales and call quality data from CFN's own systems, for CFN staff only.

How we use it

Only to show CFN's owners and managers how the business and each sales rep are doing. We do not sell, rent, or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How we protect it

Data is stored in a private database with access limited to the site's server. The QuickBooks connection key is encrypted before it is stored. Every view by a manager is logged.

Your choices

An owner can disconnect QuickBooks at any time on the Finance page, or in QuickBooks under Apps. Data already pulled is removed on request.

Contact

Questions: email CFN management at your CFN contact, or write to Change Financial Network.
