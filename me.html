    Your call sign

    Every pilot flies under a call sign. On the CFN floor, so do you.

    • It's how the floor sees you. When you close, the team board lights up with your call sign. Your real name never shows there.
    • Make it yours. Ideas: Viper, Ghost, Blaze, Ace, Closer Nation, Night Hawk.
    • The rules: 2 to 16 letters, numbers, spaces or dashes. No two pilots share one. Keep it clean.
    • Not stuck with it. Change it here any time.

    The board shows your call sign, your deals, and each deal's size. Never your pay or client names.
