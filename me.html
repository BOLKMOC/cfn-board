CallsScorecard

Best practices scorecard

Layer 1: the owner's rules (no outcome promises, a target payment before the price, longest program only and remove a card to lower the price, ask for the close, credit dip told up front) and compliance. Pass = no rule broken and no flags on the call. Layer 2: the 4 areas, 1 to 5 from scored calls. Top closer = most net debt this month. Conversion = net deals per 100 Vici calls. Gaps are against the top closer.

Closers who say X get Ylast 3 months

Real lines scored 4 or 5 in each area, with that closer's conversion. The top closer's lines come first.

Is the playbook backed by results?

For each check: scored calls where the closer did it against calls where they skipped it (last 90 days). "Deal" = the client's Forth file was submitted; "1st dep" = the first deposit cleared. A check is only proven when both sides have enough calls.

Calibration

Each quarter managers rescore calls (open a call, "Your scores"). This shows how often the scorer agrees with managers.

Call QA

Closer calls from Vici, pulled after 6 PM Pacific, Monday to Saturday. Calls of 5 minutes or longer are transcribed and scored (up to 40 a night, shared across closers) on the CFN checks. Tap a call for the full review. Recordings are kept 90 days.
