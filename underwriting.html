3. The quote

The 3 rulesevery call

  1. No guaranteed results or outcome promises. Never "guaranteed," a set savings, a settlement amount or date, or a credit score date.
  2. Target payment, then the longest program only. Before any price, ask what monthly payment they could comfortably handle. Quote the program shown above. If it's over their target, remove a credit card (smallest first, two at most). Never requote a shorter length.
  3. Ask for the close, and tell them up front their credit will dip while the accounts are being settled.
